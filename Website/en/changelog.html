Changelog

What's new in each Mordheim Ledger update, in order. Current version: 0.1.

0.1

In development
  • First public version of Mordheim Ledger.
  • Warband management: recruitment, equipment, injuries, an experience track faithful to the paper warband sheet.
  • Full, editable Codex: warbands, Trading Post, skills, special rules, mutations, mounts, spells, injuries.
  • Official, modified or custom content, clearly marked for every Codex entry.
  • Track several warbands and campaigns in parallel.
  • Two visual palettes (Ash & Warpstone, Shadow & Gold) and light/dark theme.
  • French/English translation.
  • Windows installer and Android APK, 100% offline.
