Mordheim Ledger

The ledger for your warbands of raiders and scavengers across the ruins of Mordheim.

Unofficial community app: a tool to manage your warbands, your rules catalog and your campaigns, true to the original Games Workshop tabletop game.

Download Explore the features

Features

Everything you need to lead your warband through Mordheim, from creation to campaign.

🛡️

Warband management

Recruit warriors, track their experience on a progress track faithful to the paper warband sheet, manage equipment and injuries.

📖

Full Codex

Warbands, Trading Post, skills, special rules, mutations, mounts, spells, injuries — official content, editable and extensible.

🗺️

Campaign tracking

Organize several warbands and campaigns in parallel, find their history at a glance.

🔒

100% offline

No internet connection required: your data stays stored locally, on your device.
